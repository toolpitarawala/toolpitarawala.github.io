Why ToolPitara?

Most tool websites ask you to upload your files to their server and create an account before you can do a ten-second job. We think that is backwards. Every ToolPitara tool runs entirely in your web browser: the calculation happens on your phone or computer, and your numbers, photos and files are never sent to us — we could not see them even if we wanted to.

We are starting with the calculators people in India reach for most — loan EMI, SIP growth and GST — written the way we would explain them to a friend: the formula in the open, a worked example with real numbers, and honest words about what the result does and does not mean.
