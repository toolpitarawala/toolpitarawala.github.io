Why real figures can differ

  • Loans (EMI Calculator): your bank's sanction letter governs. Processing fees, insurance premiums, floating interest rates, part-payments and the bank's own rounding and day-count conventions can all change the actual EMI and total interest.
  • Investments (SIP Calculator): the calculator grows your money at one steady rate that you choose. Real markets do not behave that way — returns vary year to year and can be negative. A higher assumed return produces a prettier number, not a safer plan.
  • Tax (GST Calculator): GST rates, exemptions and rules are set by the GST Council and change through official notifications. Place of supply, HSN/SAC classification, reverse charge, the composition scheme and input tax credit conditions can all change what a correct invoice looks like.

Verify before you decide

Before acting on any figure from this site — signing a loan agreement, starting or stopping an investment, raising an invoice, or filing a return — verify it with the source that will actually govern the transaction:

  • your bank or lender's official offer and amortisation schedule;
  • a SEBI-registered investment adviser for investment decisions;
  • a chartered accountant for tax positions and filings; and
  • official sources — the GST portal and GST Council notifications for tax rates, and the Reserve Bank of India for lending rules — where a rule, rate or slab matters.
