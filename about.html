About ToolPitara

A small, independent tools website from India, built around one simple idea: a ten-second calculation should not cost you your privacy.

What the name means

A pitara is a traditional box — often cloth-covered, sometimes brass-bound — in which a family keeps useful, treasured things: documents, tools, odds and ends that are needed again and again. We borrowed the word because that is what this site aims to be for the internet: one dependable box of everyday tools, easy to open, with everything in its place.

Who the tools are for

ToolPitara is written first for the people around us in India, in the situations we see every week:

  • A family working out whether a home-loan EMI truly fits next month's budget — not just this month's.
  • A young earner starting a first SIP and wanting to see, honestly, what small monthly amounts can and cannot do.
  • A shopkeeper or freelancer preparing a bill and needing the CGST/SGST split to be right, not roughly right.
  • Students and job applicants facing online forms that demand a photo in an exact size — tools for that are on our list next.

Nothing here is India-only, though. A loan instalment or a tax breakup is the same arithmetic anywhere; anyone is welcome to use every tool, free.

How our tools work — and why it matters

Every ToolPitara tool runs entirely inside your web browser. When you type a loan amount or a price, the maths happens on your own phone or computer. Your numbers are not uploaded to a server, not saved in a database, and not visible to us. There are no accounts to create and nothing to install.

We chose this deliberately. Many tool websites process your files and figures on their servers, where you have to trust their security and their promises. Our design removes the question altogether: what never reaches us can never leak from us. Our Privacy Policy describes this in full, tool by tool.

What we promise, and what we do not

  • Free, without tricks. The tools are free to use. There are no fake buttons, no “download” links that lead somewhere else, and tools that are not ready yet are labelled “Coming soon” instead of pretending to work.
  • Honest numbers. Each calculator shows its formula and a worked example with real figures, so you can check us with pen and paper.
  • Estimates, clearly labelled. A calculator is a planning aid, not a bank, a fund house or a chartered accountant. We say so on every page, because money decisions deserve that respect.
  • Built one tool at a time. We would rather have three tools that are correct than thirty that are almost correct. Each tool is tested before it is listed as live.

What is live today

Three calculators are ready and tested: the EMI Calculator, the SIP Calculator and the GST Calculator. Alongside them, the Photo & Signature Resizer is live, for the exact-KB photo and signature that exam and job forms ask for. In preparation — and clearly marked as such on the homepage — are tools for PDF merging, CGPA conversion and QR codes.

Who runs ToolPitara

ToolPitara is run independently from India. It is not a registered company and does not pretend to be one; it is a small website maintained with care, and it earns nothing from your data because it collects none. To cover its costs, the site has applied to show Google AdSense advertising; as promised, the Privacy Policy was updated first, and it explains exactly what that means for you.

Found a mistake in a calculation, or have a tool you wish existed? We genuinely want to hear it — write to us through the Contact page.
