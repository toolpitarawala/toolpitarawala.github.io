Contact Us

The simplest way to reach us is email — no forms, no accounts.

Write to us

Email: toolpitara@gmail.com

Clicking the address opens your own email app with the “To” field filled in. Nothing is sent through this website itself — the message travels from your email account to ours, like any normal email.

What to write about

  • A calculation looks wrong. Please tell us which tool, what you typed in, and what you expected. Wrong numbers are the one thing we most want to hear about, and reports like yours are how they get fixed.
  • A tool you wish existed. If a small everyday task keeps costing you time — a form, a conversion, a breakup of figures — describe it. Our next tools come from exactly these emails.
  • A privacy question. Ask us anything about how the site handles (or rather, does not handle) your information. The short answer is in our Privacy Policy; for anything it does not cover, email us.
  • Anything else about the site — a broken page, confusing words, a suggestion to make a tool clearer.

When will you hear back?

ToolPitara is run independently by a very small operation, not a support department. We read every message and reply to most within a few days; calculation-error reports are answered first. If your matter is urgent and financial — a loan about to be signed, a tax notice with a deadline — please do not wait on us: speak directly to your bank or a chartered accountant.

One request

Please do not email us your personal financial documents, account numbers, passwords or identity documents. Our tools are designed so you never need to share such things with us — your figures belong in the calculator on your own device, not in an inbox.
