Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to your information when you use ToolPitara. The short version: almost nothing — because the site is built so that your data never reaches us.

The short version

  • Every calculator on ToolPitara runs only in your web browser, on your own device.
  • What you type into a tool is never sent to us, never uploaded, and never stored on any server.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
  • We set no cookies ourselves. (The Google AdSense code described below is run by Google and may use cookies.)
  • We use no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • We show no advertisements yet. The site does load Google's AdSense code, so Google can verify this site and review it for advertising; if Google approves the site, advertisements will appear. Google — not us — may use cookies for those ads (explained below).
  • The only thing saved anywhere is your light/dark theme choice, stored on your own device (explained below).

The one thing stored: your theme preference

If you switch the site between light and dark mode, your browser saves that single choice in a storage area called localStorage, under the name “tp-theme” (the value is simply the word “light” or “dark”). It exists only so the site remembers your preference on your next visit. It is stored on your device only, is never sent to us or anyone else, and contains nothing about you or your calculations.

You can delete it at any time: clear “site data” for this website in your browser settings, and the preference is gone. The site will simply use its default theme again. localStorage is not a cookie, and ToolPitara itself sets no cookies at all.

Accounts, analytics, advertising and third parties

  • Accounts: There are none. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any password on this site.
  • Analytics: We run no analytics service (no Google Analytics or similar). We do not count your visits, record your clicks, or build any profile of you.
  • Advertising: We have applied to show advertising through Google AdSense to cover the site's running costs. Google's AdSense code is loaded on every page so Google can verify that we own this site and review it. As of the date at the top of this page, the site is under review and shows no advertisements yet; ads will appear only if Google approves the site. Google may use cookies to serve and personalise ads — see “Advertising cookies and your choices” below.
  • Third-party services: Apart from Google's AdSense code described above, the site loads no fonts, scripts, images or embeds from other companies, and uses no third-party APIs. Every other file that makes the site work is served with the site itself.

Hosting and technical logs

Like nearly every website, ToolPitara is delivered by a hosting provider, and web hosts normally keep short-lived technical server logs (such as the IP address a page was delivered to and the time of the request) to operate and secure their service. We do not use any such logs to identify, track or profile visitors, and we never see the numbers you type into a tool — those are never transmitted in the first place.

If you email us

If you write to us at toolpitara@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message — that is simply how email works. We use it only to read and reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not sell or share email addresses. Please never send us passwords, account numbers or identity documents; our tools are designed so you never need to share them.

Children

ToolPitara's calculators are general-purpose tools meant for adults managing money and for students doing arithmetic. The site is not directed at children, and — since the site collects no personal information from anyone — it collects none from children either. Younger students should use the finance calculators as learning aids, with a parent or teacher, and not as a basis for real money decisions.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to show, correct or delete. The theme preference lives on your own device and you can remove it yourself as explained above. If you emailed us and would like that email thread deleted, write to us from the same address and we will delete it and confirm when done. If you believe this policy does not match something the site actually does, please tell us — accuracy here matters to us.

Advertising cookies and your choices

Because this site carries Google's AdSense code, Google may place and read cookies in your browser — or use similar technologies — to serve ads, to measure how ads perform, and (where you allow it) to personalise the ads you see. How Google uses information from sites that use its services is explained in Google's own words here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

  • Personalised ads: You can turn off ad personalisation in your Google account at Google Ads Settings, and opt out of many advertising cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Cookies in general: You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculators will keep working either way.
  • Analytics: We still run no analytics. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first, before any analytics code is added — in the same way this page was updated when the AdSense code was added.
  • Your figures stay private: Advertising never changes how the calculators treat your input: figures you type into a tool are still processed only in your browser and are never sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the revised version on this page and update the date at the top. Significant changes — especially anything involving data being collected where it was not before — will also be noted on the homepage.

Contact for privacy questions

Questions about this policy or about your information: email toolpitara@gmail.com. ToolPitara is an independent website run from India; email is our contact channel for all matters, including privacy.
